Console models for plumnin's 3D library view
============================================

All of them are made smaller for plumnin by tools/models/prepare.py from the files their
authors published: only the meshes the shelf shows are kept (each cartridge in a file of
its own), the textures are shrunk to 1024 pixels and saved as JPEG (PNG where they have
transparency), and the metallic/roughness, occlusion and emission textures are left out.

gameboy.glb, gameboy-cartridge.glb, gameboycolor-cartridge.glb
  "Nintendo Game Boy (Original 1989)" by lokeig (https://sketchfab.com/lokeig).
  Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/nintendo-game-boy-original-1989-ad2f6be906e948f793fe722bbae5d29c
  License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 (CC BY-NC 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/
  NonCommercial: these three files may not be used for commercial purposes. plumnin is
  free; whoever wants to sell it has to leave them out (plumnin then draws a simple Game
  Boy of its own) or put other models in their place.
  Changes: the console and the cartridge in its slot split in two files; the cartridge
  wears the look of the Tetris one lying beside it, and for the Game Boy Color's games
  the same in black. plumnin prints each game's label (or
  its cover) over the cartridge's own.

gameboycolor.glb
  "Game Boy Color" by Wikiti (https://sketchfab.com/wikiti).
  Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/game-boy-color-03c6fe28d45244d2acd55486e3cfee19
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes: textures reduced from 4096x4096 to 1024x1024; the top of a cartridge in its
  slot (only that half is modelled) cut out, leaving the slot empty for the game's.

nes.glb, nes-cartridge.glb
  "NES_OG-85" by RadioactiveAG (https://sketchfab.com/RadioactiveAG).
  Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/nes-og-85-f12c3312b9ad45f5a4a833f602358b60
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes: the console with its pad, and the cartridge (its shell and labels, not the
  board inside), in two files; the game's sleeve left out. plumnin prints each game's
  label (or its cover) over the cartridge's own.

snes.glb
  "Super nintendo" by quick_loop (https://sketchfab.com/so_O).
  Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/super-nintendo-d42c35b117974ade8842f0964f5fd59e
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes: textures reduced to 1024x1024. Its cartridge is plumnin's own, drawn in code
  (src/render/console_models.cpp).

television.glb
  "Television 01" by Gabriel Radić, from Poly Haven (the same file as plumsg's).
  Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/Television_01
  License: CC0 1.0 (public domain), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  It stands in the living room around the 3D shelf; the rest of the room (walls, floor,
  rug, window, lamp, cabinet, video recorder and poster) is drawn by plumnin's own code
  (src/render/room.cpp, from plumsg).

Nintendo, Game Boy, Game Boy Color, NES, Super Nintendo and their logos are Nintendo's
trademarks.

Your own models: put a .glb with the same name in the "models" folder next to
plumnin.ini and plumnin uses it instead.
